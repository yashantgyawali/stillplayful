hi, i am yashant.

still playful started as a reminder to myself.

i am 30 now. when i was a kid, 30 was where you figured it all out. a grown up with a plan, a direction, everything sorted. i pictured a station with a big sign on it that said “you made it.”

i got here. there is no sign.

i have no idea what’s ahead. but i’m happy. i’m joyful. i’m pretty much the same guy i was 15 years ago, the one who put play above everything else.

turns out that’s all young yashant wanted. not answers. just to still be himself and still be having a good time.

so this is a note to him: hey, we’re still us. still playful.

and maybe it’s a note to you too. i’d like to put it on something you can carry around.

so where should it live?

pick one. just one. there is no wrong answer and no sign waiting at the end.

pick one

got it. thank you.

i’ll count it, and if yours wins, you’ll be the first to know.

still playful, coming soon.
